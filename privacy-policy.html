Privacy Policy

Spot & Steer AR: Mini Parking Challenge · Developer: Ganesh Prabhu N · Last updated 23 November 2026

Summary

Spot & Steer AR is a toy-parking game with a 3D Drive mode and an AR Mode. It has no accounts of its own, does not collect personal information itself, and does not send gameplay data to us. It shows ads from Google AdMob (see Advertising), and has an optional leaderboard through Google Play Games (see Leaderboard).

Camera

The app uses your camera only in AR Mode, to show the miniature car park over the live image in real time. Camera images are not recorded, stored or transmitted by the app. 3D Drive does not use the camera.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your level progress, coin balance and settings in a file in the app's private storage on your device. This file never leaves your device through the app, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data we collect

None on our own servers: the app has no analytics, no accounts of its own, no location access and no microphone access. The optional leaderboard is run by Google Play Games (see below).

Leaderboard (optional)

The app has a global leaderboard run by Google Play Games. Signing in is optional, never asked for at launch, and the whole game works without it. If you choose to sign in:

  • Google Play Games shares your Play Games player ID, gamer name and profile picture with the app, to show you on the leaderboard;
  • your total score (worked out from your stars and times) is posted to the leaderboard, where other players can see it next to your gamer name.

This data is handled by Google under its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We don't receive or store it on any server of our own. You can control your Play Games profile, its visibility and your game data in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • Full-screen ads may appear at natural breaks, for example when you start a drive or between levels, never while you are driving.
  • Some ads are shown only when you choose to watch them for an in-game reward.

To serve, measure and protect these ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID,
  • ad interactions and diagnostics.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Internet

The app uses the internet to load ads and, if you sign in, for the leaderboard. Gameplay works offline.

Children

The app is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children.

Safety

Play only in a clear indoor space, away from roads, driveways, traffic, stairs and real vehicles.

Changes and contact
